Randilen Map – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Randilen Map (package org.randilen.navigation) is a map and navigation app for the Randilen Wildlife Management Area. It is operated by Honeyguide on behalf of Randilen. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app uses

  • Location. If you allow it, the app uses your precise or approximate location to show where you are on the map. Your location is used on your phone to draw your position. The app does not send it to Randilen's servers and does not store it.
  • Places, favourites and settings. Places you mark, favourites and your settings (for example which map layers are on) are stored only on your phone. You can remove them by deleting them in the app or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • Random app ID. The app creates a random ID for each installation. It is sent, with the app version, when the app downloads places and road conditions from navigation.randilen.org. We use it only to count how many phones use the app. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or device identifiers.
  • Offline maps. Map tiles you view may be saved on your phone so the map works without a signal.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or microphone or camera data. It contains no advertising.

Third-party services

  • Mapbox provides the map. When the map loads, Mapbox receives map requests, your IP address and basic device information, under its own policy: mapbox.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics) may collect app usage and device information, such as app version, device model and an app instance ID, to help us understand how the app is used. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Randilen Map admin (navigation.randilen.org) supplies the places, roads and safety notices. It receives your IP address and the random app ID when the app contacts it.

Permissions

  • Location (fine and coarse): to show your position on the map.
  • Internet and network state: to load the map, places and notices.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. Data on your phone stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. The random app ID counts are kept only as long as needed to report usage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
